St. Moritz

Winter in St. Moritz and the Engadin: skiing, cross-country and events on your doorstep

St. Moritz sits at 1,856 m in the Upper Engadin and is one of the sunniest places in Switzerland – in winter, near-guaranteed snow meets a deep blue sky. Twice host of the Winter Olympics (1928 and 1948), it remains a meeting point for winter sports, culture and good food.

Skiing on Corviglia, Corvatsch and Diavolezza

The Chantarella funicular is a six-minute walk from the apartment and takes you straight into the Corviglia ski area. Together with Corvatsch and Diavolezza, around 350 km of slopes await – from wide sunny pistes to the glacier.

Cross-country skiing in the Engadin

The Engadin is one of the best cross-country regions in Europe: over 200 km of groomed trails, many of them flat across the frozen lakes.

Winter hiking and sledging

Groomed winter trails run along the lakes and through the Staz forest. The Preda–Bergün sledge run and the descent from Muottas Muragl are among the longest in Switzerland.

Summer and autumn in St. Moritz and the Engadin: mountains, lakes and golden larches

In summer the Upper Engadin becomes a high plateau of lakes: sailors and surfers on Lake Silvaplana, hiking trails between 1,800 and 3,000 metres, flow trails for bikers and, in October, the golden larch forests the valley is famous for. The mountain railways from St. Moritz Dorf are a six-minute walk from the apartment.

Hiking on three levels

From the lakeshore via forest paths to the high trails on Corviglia and Muottas Muragl: 580 kilometres of marked trails, many reachable by cable car. The lakes walk from St. Moritz to Maloja is the classic.

Biking on Corviglia

The Engadin is one of the great bike regions of the Alps: flow trails from the Corviglia top station, the Suvretta Loop and the Bernina trail to Poschiavo. Bikes and e-bikes can be rented in the village.

Lakes: sailing, surfing, swimming

The Maloja wind makes Lake Silvaplana a playground for sailors, wind- and kitesurfers. For a swim head to Lej da Staz in the larch forest, for a stroll to the lakeside promenade in St. Moritz.

Golden autumn

From early October the larch forests of the Engadin turn golden – two weeks when the valley is at its most beautiful: clear air, quiet trails and the first snow on the peaks.

Our favourite tours

Four tours we keep coming back to – times are approximate, details on the linked official pages.

Hiking · September–October · approx. 15 km · 650 m ascent

Lagh da Saoseo in autumn

The deep-blue mountain lake in Val da Camp (Poschiavo valley) is considered one of the most beautiful in the Alps – in October, when the larches glow, it is our favourite. By train over the Bernina and PostBus to Sfazù; from there via Rifugio Saoseo to the lake and on to Lagh da Val Viola. Easier option with the shuttle bus to Alp Camp.

· Tour description on graubuenden.ch
Mountain bike · loop from Celerina · 34 km · 1,150 m ascent · approx. 4 h

Bike: Suvretta Loop over the Suvretta Pass

From Celerina up the gravel road, about 400 metres of climbing to Alp Laret, past Lej Alv to the Corviglia top station (2,400 m) and over the last steep metres onto the trail around Piz Nair to the Suvretta Pass, the highest point. Then the five-kilometre Suvretta da Samedan singletrack down to Alp Suvretta, along the alpine path through Val Bever to Spinas (Engadin nut cake at the mountain inn) and via Bever back along the valley to Celerina. Technically demanding, physically moderate.

· Route 671 on graubuenden.ch
Hiking · summer · approx. 5 h · 1,250 m ascent

Piz Nair via the Suvretta Pass

From St. Moritz Dorf through the quiet Val Suvretta up to Lej Suvretta and over the Suvretta Pass (2,615 m) to Piz Nair (3,056 m) – at the top, the panorama over the Upper Engadin lakes and the Bernina range, plus the summit restaurant. Back the easy way by Piz Nair cable car and Corviglia funicular to your doorstep.

· Piz Nair cable car on myswitzerland.com
Mountain bike · enduro · 38 km · 950 m up, 1,600 m down

Bike: Bernina downhill to Poschiavo

From St. Moritz via Pontresina up to the Bernina Pass (2,328 m), past Lago Bianco to Alp Grüm with its view of the Palü glacier, then down the singletrack along the railway line to Poschiavo – a trail the International Mountain Bike Association calls “epic”. Gelato on the piazza, then back over the pass on the Bernina Express (bikes carried).

· Route 673 on graubuenden.ch

Getting here

Getting to St. Moritz in winter

By car over the Julier Pass, by Rhaetian Railway along the Albula line, or by air to Zurich or Samedan – the apartment is easy to reach whichever way you travel.

By car over the Julier Pass

From Zurich about 3 hours (via Chur and the Julier Pass, open all year), from Milan about 2.5 hours (Maloja or Bernina Pass), from Munich about 4 hours. Parking: an underground parking space in the building is included in the rent. A second garage space can be rented on request; alternatively, the public Quadrellas car park is a 5-minute walk away.

By Rhaetian Railway (Albula line)

From Zurich main station, changing in Chur, in about 3 hours 20 minutes – the Rhaetian Railway’s Albula line is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the most beautiful way into the Engadin. From St. Moritz station by bus to the Serlas stop (3-minute walk to the apartment) or in 4 minutes by taxi. Luggage and skis: lift to the 3rd floor; ski equipment is stored in the ski room in the building.

By plane: Zurich or Engadin Airport Samedan

The nearest international airport is Zurich, about 3.5 hours by car or train (direct rail link from the airport via Chur). Engadin Airport in Samedan is 12 minutes by car and open to private and charter flights; taxis and rental cars available locally. On request we are happy to arrange a transfer from Zurich Airport.

Getting to St. Moritz in summer

By car over the passes, by Rhaetian Railway along the Albula line or by air to Zurich or Samedan – the apartment is easy to reach whichever way you come.

By car over the passes

From Zurich about 3 hours via Chur and the Julier Pass; in summer the Albula and Flüela passes are open too. From Milan about 2.5 hours via the Maloja or Bernina Pass, from Munich about 4 hours. Parking: one underground parking space in the building is included in the rent. A second garage space can be rented on request; alternatively, the public Quadrellas car park is a 5-minute walk away.

By Rhaetian Railway (Albula line)

From Zurich main station with a change in Chur in about 3 hours 20 minutes – the Albula line of the Rhaetian Railway is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the most beautiful way into the Engadin. From St. Moritz, the Bernina Express to Tirano makes a superb day trip. From St. Moritz station take the bus to the Serlas stop (3-minute walk to the apartment) or a 4-minute taxi ride.

By air: Zurich or Engadin Airport Samedan

The nearest international airport is Zurich, about 3.5 hours by car or train (direct rail link from the airport via Chur). Engadin Airport in Samedan is 12 minutes away by car and open to private and charter flights; taxis or rental cars on site. On request we are happy to arrange a transfer from Zurich Airport.
